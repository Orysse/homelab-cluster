  hi. you found a plain text website. in this economy.

  the story: i wanted to try microvm.nix.
  so, naturally, i wiped a perfectly good mini PC, bought a domain,
  and built an entire kubernetes cluster to serve you this .txt file.

  totally proportionate. no regrets. some regrets.


  wait, what's microvm.nix?
  -------------------------

  tiny virtual machines, described in a few lines of Nix.
  no disk image to babysit: the config IS the machine.
  delete one, rebuild it, it comes back exactly the same.
  like a phoenix. a very nerdy phoenix.

  fun facts
  ---------

  * 3 VMs, 1 PC, 0 clicks: it's all code.
  * the VMs live in RAM. they forget everything when they reboot.
    honestly, relatable.
  * this page has better uptime than my sleep schedule.
  * a random pod just served you this. you can even ask which one:

        curl -sI https://abe.lc | grep -i x-served-by


  no cookies. no javascript. no tracking. just vibes.
